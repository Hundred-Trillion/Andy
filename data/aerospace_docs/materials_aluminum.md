# Aluminum 7075-T6 — Aerospace Structural Alloy

## Overview
Aluminum 7075-T6 is the most widely used high-strength aluminum alloy in aerospace structures. It belongs to the 7xxx series (Al-Zn-Mg-Cu) and achieves its exceptional strength through precipitation hardening (T6 temper).

## Mechanical Properties
- **Density**: 2.81 g/cm³ (0.102 lb/in³)
- **Yield Strength (0.2%)**: 503 MPa (73 ksi)
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength**: 572 MPa (83 ksi)
- **Elastic Modulus**: 71.7 GPa (10.4 Msi)
- **Shear Strength**: 331 MPa
- **Fatigue Strength** (500M cycles): 159 MPa (23 ksi)
- **Elongation at Break**: 11%
- **Poisson's Ratio**: 0.33
- **Hardness**: 150 HB (Brinell)

## Aerospace Applications
7075-T6 is used extensively in aircraft fuselage frames, wing spars, bulkheads, and rocket motor casings. Its high strength-to-weight ratio (specific strength ~204 kN·m/kg) makes it ideal for primary structural members where weight is critical.

Common applications include:
- Wing skins and stringers
- Fuselage frames and longerons
- Rocket interstage adapters
- Fin attachment hardware
- Structural brackets and fittings

## Design Considerations
- **Corrosion**: 7075 has poor corrosion resistance compared to 6061. Anodizing or cladding (Alclad) is recommended for exposed surfaces.
- **Weldability**: Poor. Mechanical fastening (rivets, bolts) is preferred. Friction stir welding is possible but reduces strength in the HAZ.
- **Machinability**: Excellent. One of the best aluminum alloys for CNC machining.
- **Temperature Limit**: Properties degrade above 120°C (250°F). Not suitable for high-temperature applications.
